⚾ Pitch Launcher

Last: 0 cmBest: 0 cm$0

Move the mouse (or ↑/↓) to aim your throw. Click or press Space to start the power meter, then again to release the pitch. Earn money by distance and buy upgrades. Don’t overwork your pitcher: his arm can give out after a random number of throws, and you lose all your money and upgrades when it does. Visit the Dojo to train and last longer. Press R to reset all progress. Dojo cat: the cat wanders around the Dojo, naps, plays in the ice bath or with a plyo ball (about 30 seconds each), and sometimes climbs the banner and falls. His name tag follows him around, and you can change the name, his color and his spots in Customize. On a phone: drag on the field to aim, lift your finger to start the power meter, then tap to throw.
